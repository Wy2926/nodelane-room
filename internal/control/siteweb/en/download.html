Choose the right file to get started.

Check the version and platform

Follow the release notes to choose a full desktop package. CLI development archives do not include a graphical interface. Node software is not a substitute for the player client.

Keep your device identity

Regular upgrades preserve your linked account and device identity. When reinstalling, use the full package and follow the instructions for your platform.

Start with diagnostics

The app includes network diagnostics. Include your operating system version, app version, and error code when reporting a problem to help identify the cause.
