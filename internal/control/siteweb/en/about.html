ABOUT / NODELANE ROOM

Connection means
doing what you love, together.

NodeLane Room is NodeLane's game networking product.
We want to make LAN games with friends easier to start.

OUR APPROACH

A simple room,
with the connection handled.

From sending an invitation to seeing friends in the member list, getting together should feel clear and connected. NodeLane Room organizes game connections around rooms, so players can find their friends and administrators can understand their service.

We value clear status and well-defined capabilities. Unknown connections are shown as unknown; when something needs your attention, the client gives you a next step. Product capabilities, code and validation results are maintained in the project.

01 / EXPERIENCE

Designed around playing together

Creating rooms, sending invitations, checking members and diagnosing connections belong in the places you need them. Familiar desktop controls help you spend less time moving between screens.

02 / TRANSPARENCY

Show what is actually happening

Supported platforms, game compatibility and connection requirements are explained clearly. Interface examples and actual test results are identified separately.

03 / IMPROVEMENT

Give feedback a clear destination

The project repository brings code, documentation and issue reports together. Reproducible problems and specific suggestions help guide improvements.
