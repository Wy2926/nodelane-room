TRUST / PRIVACY

Understand how
your data is used.

This page explains how the current product uses data.
For a self-hosted service, ask its provider about operation and retention policies.

Updated September 12, 2026

Visiting this website

The public website does not set login cookies or use advertising trackers, third-party analytics scripts or external fonts. The server and reverse proxy may keep access logs according to their configuration, including IP addresses, request paths and times. External sites such as GitHub apply their own data practices when you follow a link to them.

Accounts and devices

The game service stores account identifiers, display names, linked devices, session expiry times and authorization status to support sign-in, identify members and revoke devices. Registered accounts sign in through the configured identity service, whose own data handling is covered by its notices. Administrator sessions use necessary cookies and are separate from the public website pages.

Rooms and networking

The service processes rooms, members, invitation status and network authorization to establish connections within a room. Administrators can view the member, device and room information needed to operate the service, and administrative actions are recorded in an audit log. Device identity is separate from tunnel keys; short-lived certificates limit the duration of connection authorization.

Connection diagnostics

Network status may include virtual IPs, observed public endpoints, connection types, latency, packet loss and traffic. The current control instance keeps the latest 60 seconds of live monitoring samples and clears them on restart. This does not mean account, room or audit records are cleared at the same time. IP region information is looked up in a local database; member IPs are not sent to third parties for those lookups.

Managing your data

You can view your account and authorized devices in the client and use the available controls to sign out a device. Contact your actual service provider about account deletion, backend records, access logs or backup retention. Technical issues can be submitted through the project feedback channel. Do not include credentials or other private information in public reports.

Updates to this notice

This page and the project documentation are maintained as product behavior changes. A deployment may use separate identity services, reverse proxies and retention settings; read the corresponding service notices alongside this page.
