KNOW YOUR CONNECTION

An honest view of your connection.

The right games and network conditions
make a good place to start.

Designed for LAN games

Your game needs to support LAN or direct IP connections and use network rules allowed by the server. Compatibility depends on testing with the specific game and platform.

The route depends on your network

Devices try to connect directly and use an available relay when needed. Your network must allow UDP; connectivity cannot be guaranteed on every network.

Only confirmed status

Connection types come from actual tunnels; latency and packet loss come from probes. Website screenshots demonstrate the interface, not live network performance.
