Understand the product's purpose, requirements and support boundaries.

Updated September 12, 2026

Product purpose

NodeLane Room provides game LAN connections for invited members within a room. It does not provide game content, game accounts or an internet exit proxy, and does not replace a game's own licensing or services.

Conditions of use

Install the software on devices and networks you are authorized to use and manage. Use lawfully obtained games and follow the rules of the game and network providers. Share invitations only with people you want to play with, and protect your device identity and account.

Compatibility and availability

Connections depend on game protocols, operating systems, UDP reachability, node status and room rules. Some games require direct IP connections or server configuration. Supported capabilities are described in the product documentation and actual test results; compatibility with every network, game or device is not guaranteed.

Versions and updates

Get the complete installer for your platform from a trusted release channel and read its release notes. In-app updates require valid update trust and an available release configuration. System installation, driver setup and upgrades may require administrator permission.

Room and account management

Rooms have capacity and expiry limits. Hosts can manage invitations and members; service administrators can manage accounts, devices and rooms under the service's rules. Signing out a device, authorization expiry, room closure or member removal may end the corresponding connections.

The project and third-party components

Third-party components and their license information are included with the source and installation packages. See the third-party notices. Game and third-party product names belong to their respective rights holders. Mentioning a compatibility scenario does not imply a partnership or endorsement.

Support and feedback

Start with the help center for usage questions and report technical defects in the project repository. For a self-hosted or third-party-operated service, refer to that provider's published information about its operator, service terms and support channels.
